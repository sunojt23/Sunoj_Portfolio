Inventory Management & Demand Forecasting System

Personal Project · Sep 2023 — Nov 2023

A data-driven inventory pipeline identifying optimal safety-stock levels and reorder points, reducing stockouts by 30%. Built and benchmarked ETL workflows feeding Prophet and ARIMA forecasting models for SKU-level demand prediction, achieving a 20% improvement in accuracy via MAPE-based evaluation. Visualization layers in Matplotlib/Seaborn surfaced seasonal demand patterns to drive inventory-optimization decisions.

Stack: Python, Pandas, NumPy, Prophet, ARIMA, Matplotlib, Seaborn
