ServiceNow AI Resolution Assistant (RAG)

Applied GenAI project

An end-to-end RAG assistant that retrieves relevant historical ServiceNow tickets and generates contextual resolution recommendations for incoming support requests. Past tickets are ingested, preprocessed, embedded, and vector-indexed into a searchable knowledge base; an LLM then answers from the retrieved context, keeping responses grounded — reducing repetitive manual triage and automating roughly 70% of routine ticket queries.

Stack: Python, OpenAI Embeddings, Vector Search, RAG, LLM

02

Inventory Management & Demand Forecasting

2023

A Python ETL pipeline that ingests sales and inventory data from SAP over a REST API, then cleans, transforms, and engineers demand features — seasonality, trend, and reorder signals — for SKU-level forecasting. Prophet and ARIMA were benchmarked with MAPE-based evaluation to select the best forecast, improving accuracy by 20% and identifying safety-stock and reorder points that cut stockouts by 30%, with Matplotlib/Seaborn visuals of seasonal demand patterns.

Stack: Python, REST APIs, Pandas, NumPy, Prophet, ARIMA, Matplotlib, Seaborn
